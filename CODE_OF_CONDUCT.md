# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and free from harassment. Helpful behavior includes giving actionable feedback, accepting correction gracefully, and keeping technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this standard.

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
